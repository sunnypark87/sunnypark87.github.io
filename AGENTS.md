# Repository instructions

## Littlebread Lab brand context

Before proposing or implementing branding, content structure, visual design, UI, layouts, or components, read and follow [`docs/brand-guidelines.md`](docs/brand-guidelines.md).

- Treat that document as the repository's highest-level brand and visual-design context.
- Preserve its distinction between confirmed principles, changeable current directions, and undecided details.
- Do not turn an undecided UI or information-architecture choice into a project standard without a separate design decision.
- Explain design proposals in terms of the documented brand principles, not aesthetics alone.
